WenSolata Mono 慰文楷 Web Fonts

WenSolata Mono is a monospaced font family with Inconsolata Latin glyphs and LXGW WenKai Mono Lite Chinese glyphs.

Light: The quick brown fox jumps over 1234567890. 中文示例。

Regular: The quick brown fox jumps over 1234567890. 中文示例。

Bold: The quick brown fox jumps over 1234567890. 中文示例。

Use the stylesheet

Add this to an HTML document:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://hanlhe.github.io/WenSolata-Mono/WenSolataMono.css">

Then set font-family: "WenSolata Mono", monospace; in CSS. Use font-weight: 300 for Light, 400 for Regular, or 700 for Bold.
