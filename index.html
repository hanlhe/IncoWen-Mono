WenSolata Mono 慰文楷

WenSolata Mono is a monospaced font family with Inconsolata Latin glyphs and LXGW WenKai Mono Lite Chinese glyphs.

Light — 道德经 · Tao Te Ching

上善若水。水善利万物而不争，处众人之所恶，故几于道。

The highest goodness is like water. Water benefits all things without contention, dwelling where others disdain, and thus comes near to the Tao.

Regular — 逍遥游 · Zhuangzi

北冥有鱼，其名为鲲。化而为鸟，其名为鹏；怒而飞，其翼若垂天之云。

In the Northern Ocean there is a fish called Kun. It transforms into a bird called Peng, whose wings in flight are like clouds hanging from the sky.

Bold — 兰亭集序 · Preface to the Orchid Pavilion

是日也，天朗气清，惠风和畅。仰观宇宙之大，俯察品类之盛，所以游目骋怀，信可乐也。

The sky was bright, the air clear, and a gentle breeze blew. Looking up at the vastness of the universe and down at the abundance of all things, we let our eyes wander and our thoughts roam freely.

Use the stylesheet

Add this to an HTML document:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://hanlhe.github.io/WenSolata-Mono/WenSolataMono.css">

Then set font-family: "WenSolata Mono", monospace; in CSS. Use font-weight: 300 for Light, 400 for Regular, or 700 for Bold.
